# Garhwali (गढ़वाली) — Uttarakhand, Garhwal region
Script: Devanagari. AI singers default to Hindi pronunciation, so keep dialect words distinctive and review with a native speaker.

## Folk forms
- **Jagar**: invocation of local deities, trance-like, building tempo
- **Mangal**: wedding songs
- **Thadya / Chaunfula / Jhumailo**: group dance songs, circular, call-and-response
- **Khuder / Bajuband**: songs of longing and separation (migration from pahad is a classic theme)

## Instruments
Dhol-Damau (core rhythm), Masakbeen (bagpipe), Hurka, Ransingha / Turri (horns), Bansuri, Daunr-Thali

## Fusion ideas
- Dhol-damau groove over 808 sub-bass, trap hats at double time
- Masakbeen riff as the hook melody, rap verse about pahad → city migration
- Typical tempo: folk dance 90–110 BPM; feels good with half-time trap

## Common themes / imagery
Pahad, gaon, ghughuti (bird), buransh (rhododendron), kafal, Bedu Pako, Nanda Devi, ghaam (sun), maiti (mother's home)
