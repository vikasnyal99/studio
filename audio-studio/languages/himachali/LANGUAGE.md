# Himachali / Pahari (हिमाचली) — Himachal Pradesh
Varies by region (Kullui, Mandyali, Kangri, Chambyali…). Pick one dialect per song.

## Folk forms
- **Nati**: signature group dance (Kullu Nati especially), lilting slow-to-fast groove
- **Jhoori**: romantic songs
- **Laman**: call-and-response love songs (great duet hook)
- **Sanskar songs**: wedding and ritual songs

## Instruments
Nagara, Dhol, Karnal & Ransingha (long horns), Shehnai, Bansuri, Narsingha

## Fusion ideas
- Nati groove + deep house / afro-house kick (works naturally)
- Karnal blast as drop impact, shehnai lead over synth pads
- Typical tempo 100–125 BPM

## Imagery
Deodar trees, snow, apple orchards, Kullu Dussehra, devta, topi, pattu shawl
