# Kumaoni (कुमाऊँनी) — Uttarakhand, Kumaon region
Script: Devanagari. Distinct from Garhwali; don't mix vocabulary.

## Folk forms
- **Jhoda**: circular group dance, slow-to-fast, call-and-response
- **Chhapeli**: romantic duet dance songs
- **Chanchari**: festival group songs
- **Nyoli**: songs of longing, sung in the forests
- **Bair**: competitive sung debates (good fit for rap battle!)
- **Holi baithki / khadi**: Kumaoni Holi songs

## Instruments
Hudka (hourglass drum, core), Dhol-Damau, Turri, Masakbeen, Bansuri, Mashak

## Fusion ideas
- Bair → rap battle format (two voices trading lines)
- Hudka loop + boom-bap drums; Chhapeli duet as the hook
- Jhoda's acceleration → EDM build-up and drop

## Imagery
Almora, Nainital, Bal Mithai, Kafal, Ghughuti, Uttarayani, Harela, Nanda Devi
