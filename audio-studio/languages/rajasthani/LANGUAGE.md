# Rajasthani (राजस्थानी) — Marwari, Mewari, Dhundhari…
Choose a dialect per song (Marwari is the most recognisable).

## Folk forms
- **Manganiyar / Langa traditions**: soaring vocal improvisation, desert ballads
- **Ghoomar**: graceful women's dance song
- **Panihari**: songs of women fetching water
- **Kalbeliya**: fast, snake-dance music (been + dholak)
- **Moomal, Kesariya Balam**: traditional love ballads (public-domain melodies, but don't copy modern recordings)

## Instruments
Kamaicha, Sindhi Sarangi, Khartal, Morchang (jaw harp), Algoza, Dholak, Ravanhatta, Been, Pungi

## Fusion ideas
- Khartal + morchang as the rhythmic bed under trap drums
- Kamaicha drone + ambient pad intro → beat drop
- Desert "cinematic" style: long alaap → rap verse

## Imagery
Dhora (dunes), oont (camel), kesariya, padharo, baisa, havelis, Thar, saavan
