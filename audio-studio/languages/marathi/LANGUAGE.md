# Marathi (मराठी) — Maharashtra
Well supported by AI vocal models; a strong existing Marathi rap/hip-hop scene.

## Folk forms
- **Lavani**: high-energy, sensuous, dholki-driven
- **Powada**: heroic ballads (Shivaji Maharaj themes); perfect base for rap
- **Koli geet**: fisherfolk songs, bouncy 6/8 feel
- **Gondhal / Bharud / Abhang**: devotional and folk-theatre forms
- **Dhol-Tasha**: Ganeshotsav processional rhythm

## Instruments
Dholki, Dhol-Tasha, Halgi, Sambal, Tuntuni, Ektara, Lezim, Harmonium, Chonda

## Fusion ideas
- Powada storytelling → rap verses, dhol-tasha as the drop
- Lavani dholki patterns over reggaeton / dancehall drums
- Koli 6/8 bounce + afrobeat

## Imagery
Ganpati Bappa, wari, Pune / Mumbai streets, koliwada, sahyadri, bhagwa, puran poli
