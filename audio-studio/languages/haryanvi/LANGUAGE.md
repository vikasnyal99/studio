# Haryanvi (हरियाणवी) — Haryana
The most AI-friendly of the set: close to Hindi, and Haryanvi pop/rap is a big existing scene.

## Folk forms
- **Ragni**: sung story-poetry, competitive, often in a dialogue format
- **Saang**: folk theatre songs
- **Phag / Teej songs**: seasonal festival songs for women
- **Modern Haryanvi pop**: swag, desi pride, cars, thar, gym, "chhora/chhori"

## Instruments
Been (snake-charmer pipe), Dholak, Chimta, Algoza, Nagada, Khartal, Harmonium

## Fusion ideas
- Been riff + drill/trap 808s: the classic modern Haryanvi sound
- Ragni dialogue → male rap verse vs female sung response
- Tempo: 90–100 BPM (trap half-time) or 140 drill

## Vocab flavour
Thare / mhare, ghana, chhora / chhori, thaada, laadla, bairan, kasuta
