# Song Brief — Gaam Ki Rauf (गाम की रौफ)

- **Language / dialect:** Haryanvi (central / Rohtak-style)
- **Theme / story:** A village boy works in Gurgaon and wears a suit to the office, but his roots, his family and his desi pride stay with him. The village (a female voice) calls him back.
- **Mood:** energetic, proud, a little nostalgic in the hook
- **Folk base:** Ragni-style dialogue (male rap verses ↔ female folk-sung hook)
- **Modern layer:** Trap: heavy 808 bass, half-time drums, rolling hi-hats
- **BPM / time feel:** 94 BPM, half-time trap feel
- **Key / scale:** C minor with a Phrygian flavour (flat 2nd) to suit the been riff
- **Vocals:** Male rap (verses, intro, outro) + female folk vocal (pre-hook, hook, bridge alaap)
- **Signature sounds:** Been riff (hook melody), dholak fills, chimta on off-beats, sub-808 slides
- **Target length:** ~3:00
- **Use:** Commercial: generate on a paid Suno plan only

## Structure
Intro (been + spoken) → Verse 1 (rap) → Pre-Hook (female) → Hook → Verse 2 (rap) → Pre-Hook → Hook → Bridge (been solo + alaap) → Hook ×2 → Outro (spoken)
