# Lyrics — Gaam Ki Rauf (गाम की रौफ) — Haryanvi

> Status: DRAFT v1. No native Haryanvi reviewer. Words flagged at the bottom.
> Format: Devanagari | Roman | English meaning

## [Intro — spoken, male, over been riff]
हाँ भाई... हरियाणे का छोरा सै | haan bhai... Haryane ka chhora sai | Yes brother... I'm a boy from Haryana
गाम तै आया सै | gaam tai aaya sai | Came here from the village

## [Verse 1 — rap, male]
तड़के उठ के देख्या, खेत म्हं लहरावै सरसों | tadke uth ke dekhya, khet mhan lehraave sarson | Woke at dawn and saw mustard swaying in the fields
ताऊ की चौपाल पे हुक्का गुड़गुड़ावै बरसों तै | taau ki chaupal pe hukka gudgudaave barson tai | At uncle's chaupal the hookah has bubbled for years
दूध-दही का खाणा, म्हारे खून म्हं सै जोर | doodh-dahi ka khaana, mhaare khoon mhan sai jor | Milk and curd is our food, there's strength in our blood
शहर गया छोरा, पर दिल रहग्या गाम की ओर | shehar gaya chhora, par dil rehgya gaam ki or | The boy went to the city, but his heart stayed with the village

गुड़गाँव की बिल्डिंग, काँच की दीवार | Gurgaon ki building, kaanch ki deewaar | Gurgaon buildings, walls of glass
सूट-बूट पहर लिया, पर भीतर तै देसी यार | suit-boot pehar liya, par bheetar tai desi yaar | Put on a suit and boots, but inside I'm desi, friend
मीटिंग म्हं अंग्रेजी, फोन पे "के हाल सै ताऊ?" | meeting mhan angrezi, phone pe "ke haal sai taau?" | English in meetings, on the phone "How are you, uncle?"
माट्टी की खुशबू ना भूलूँ कदे, चाहे जाऊँ कितै | maatti ki khushboo na bhoolun kade, chaahe jaaun kitai | I'll never forget the smell of the soil, wherever I go

## [Pre-Hook — female, sung]
रै छोरे, तू कित सै? | re chhore, tu kit sai? | Hey boy, where are you?
गाम बुलावै तन्नै | gaam bulaave tanne | The village is calling you
पीपल की छाँ म्हं | peepal ki chhaan mhan | In the shade of the peepal tree
बाट देखूँ तेरी मैं | baat dekhun teri main | I'm waiting for you

## [Hook — female sung, male adlibs]
रौफ गाम की, छोरा देसी रे | rauf gaam ki, chhora desi re | The village swagger, a desi boy
रौफ गाम की, दिल परदेसी रे | rauf gaam ki, dil pardesi re | The village swagger, a heart far from home
लठ गाड़ दे जित भी जावै | lath gaad de jit bhi jaave | Wherever he goes, he plants his staff (makes his mark)
हरियाणे का छोरा सै, सब नै बतावै | Haryane ka chhora sai, sab nai bataave | Tells everyone he's a Haryana boy

## [Verse 2 — rap, male]
बापू कहवै, "बेटा, जड़ ना छोड़िए" | bapu kahve, "beta, jad na chhodiye" | Father says, "Son, never leave your roots"
माँ कहवै, "रोटी खा के फोन जरूर करिए" | maa kahve, "roti kha ke phone jaroor kariye" | Mother says, "Eat, and make sure you call"
भाइयाँ गैल कबड्डी खेली गाम की गळी म्हं | bhaiyan gail kabaddi kheli gaam ki gali mhan | Played kabaddi with my brothers in the village lanes
आज भी वो ही जोश सै म्हारी हर इक चाल म्हं | aaj bhi wo hi josh sai mhaari har ik chaal mhan | That same fire is in every step I take today

गाड्डी की चाबी, पर दिल तै सादा | gaaddi ki chaabi, par dil tai saada | Car keys in hand, but simple at heart
जो कह दिया इक बार, वो पक्का वादा | jo keh diya ik baar, wo pakka vaada | What I say once is a firm promise
ना घमंड, ना दिखावा, बस इज्जत अर प्यार | na ghamand, na dikhaava, bas ijjat ar pyaar | No arrogance, no show, just respect and love
यो छोरा देसी, हर गाम का यार | yo chhora desi, har gaam ka yaar | This desi boy is every village's friend

## Review notes
- Words to double-check (standard Haryanvi as far as I know, but confirm with a speaker if you can):
  म्हं (mhan = in), सै (sai = is), तै (tai = from/than), गैल (gail = with), कितै (kitai = anywhere), कदे (kade = ever), तन्नै (tanne = to you), रौफ (rauf = swagger/dominance), लठ गाड़ना (planting the staff = asserting yourself), पहर (pehar = wear)
- "देसाँ म्हं देस हरियाणा, जित दूध-दही का खाणा" is a traditional saying. Verse 1 line 3 alludes to it, which is fine.
- No caste, brand or real-person references (intentional: safer for distribution).
