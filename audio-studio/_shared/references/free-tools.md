# Tool Stack — cost & licensing (verify current terms before release)

This machine: i5-1145G7, 15 GB RAM, Intel Iris Xe (no CUDA) → heavy AI models run in the cloud, editing/mixing runs locally.

## Song generation (vocals + music)
| Tool | Cost | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| **Suno** | Free daily credits; Pro ≈ $10/mo | Best quality for Hindi-family vocals. **Free tier = non-commercial**; songs made on Pro/Premier can be monetized. Subscribe only during months you're releasing. |
| **ACE-Step** (open source) | Free on Kaggle (≈30 GPU hrs/week) or Colab T4 | Full songs with vocals from lyrics + tags; open licence. Weaker on Indian dialect pronunciation than Suno. |
| YuE / DiffRhythm (open source) | Free on Kaggle | Lyrics-to-song alternatives; experiment. |

## Vocals
| Tool | Cost | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Your own voice + Audacity | Free | Best for pronunciation; a USB mic (~₹1,500–3,000) helps a lot. |
| **Applio / RVC** | Free (train on Kaggle/Colab) | Voice conversion: sing/rap guide yourself, convert to a trained voice. **Only train on voices you have rights to** (yours / consenting singer). |

## Stems, editing, mixing, mastering
| Tool | Cost | Runs |
|------|------|------|
| Demucs (htdemucs) / Ultimate Vocal Remover | Free | Local CPU (a few min per song) |
| Audacity (installed) | Free | Local |
| Reaper | $60 licence, unlimited free trial | Local — recommended DAW |
| LMMS / Ardour | Free | Local |
| BandLab | Free | Browser — DAW + free mastering |
| Matchering | Free | Local — master against a reference track |
| Vital (synth), Spitfire LABS | Free plugins | Local |
