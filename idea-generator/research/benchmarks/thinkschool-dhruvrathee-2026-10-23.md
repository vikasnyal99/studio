# Benchmarks: Think School and Dhruv Rathee (last 40 uploads, 2026-10-23)
Raw data: `ThinkSchool.json`, `dhruvrathee.json`

## Think School (6.6M subs)
- **Format:** 14–22 min case-study explainers, plus 40–85 min podcasts / masterclasses
- **Biggest recent videos:**
  - BlackRock: 5.1M
  - Saudi "impossible city": 3.7M
  - "Dirty AI lie": 3.5M
  - Jaishankar 4F crisis: 2.5M
  - Voter list protests: 2.1M
  - BMW: 2.0M
- **Topics:** a company's rise or fall, geopolitics through an economic lens, and India's systemic problems (medical seats, Mumbai's budget, IT jobs)
- **Title formula:** `ALL-CAPS HOOK | Why/How question?`, e.g. "Strange Fall of Oracle | How a $300 Billion AI Deal Changed Everything?"

## Dhruv Rathee (33.2M subs)
- **Format:** 13–29 min, scripted, with heavy animation, maps and b-roll
- **Biggest recent videos:**
  - How Japan became a superpower: 26M
  - Bhangarh fort: 23M
  - Vitamin D/B12: 21M
  - Gyanesh Kumar exposed: 21M
  - Khan Sir: 20M
  - Dhurandhar 2 lies: 20M
- **Topic mix:** about ⅓ politics and current affairs; ⅔ evergreen mystery, history, science and health
- **Title formula:** `Reality of X` / `X EXPOSED` / `True Story` / `How X happened?`, plus "| Dhruv Rathee"

## What both channels have in common
1. **Videos are 15–25 min**, built on research and a script. Neither does on-the-spot commentary.
2. **The story is structured:** hook → stakes → backstory → twist → what it means for you.
3. **Visuals carry the story:** maps, archive clips, animated graphs, on-screen text.
4. **Most views come from evergreen topics.** Current events are only part of the mix.
5. **Packaging is strong:** curiosity titles with one CAPS word, and an emotional face plus one strong visual on the thumbnail.

## Why copying their topics fails for a small channel
YouTube shows a small channel's national-politics video to people who already watched Dhruv or Think School on the same topic. They click the big channel. Your data shows this: Bishnoi got 2.5% CTR, Gyanesh Kumar 2.9%, the Dhruv Rathee video 3.7%.
**Use their format, not their topics.** Apply the same storytelling to a region and community they don't cover: Delhi NCR, Haryana and Western UP.
