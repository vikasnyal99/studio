## 2. Brand positioning
**"NCR ki Asli Kahani"**: researched Hindi stories about Delhi NCR, Haryana, Western UP and the Gurjar community. Picture Dhruv Rathee's storytelling, focused on a region the big channels ignore.

- **Promise:** "The stories behind the people, villages and money of North India's heartland."
- **Recurring series** (so viewers have a reason to subscribe):
  1. **Legends** (an NCR personality's real story)
  2. **NCR Files** (money, land, scams, systems)
  3. **Itihaas** (history of villages, communities, heroes)
  4. **Bahas** (debates, kept balanced)
- **Visual identity:** the same thumbnail template every time (big face, 3–4 Hindi/Hinglish words, red/yellow accent, series tag in the corner) and the same opening line ("Ye kahani hai…").
- **Length path:** 8–10 min now → 12–15 min in a month → 15–20 min after that. Always write a full script.

## 5. Rules for every video
- **Script structure:** first 30s hook (most shocking fact or question) → stakes → story → twist → "what it means for you" → end with a question
- **Title:** `<Name/Place> ki asli kahani | <curiosity hook>?` with one CAPS word
- **Links:** a card plus end screen from the old viral videos to the new ones (Pepsu RIP → Pepsu Part 2, etc.)
- **Comments:** pin one asking "Agla legend kaun?" and reply to every comment in the first hour
- **What to track:** CTR (target 6%+), average % viewed (target 40%+), subscribers per 1K views (now ~0.4, target 3+)
- **Facts:** check every claim and show sources on screen. Careful, accurate research is what makes a brand on Dhruv's or Think School's level.
