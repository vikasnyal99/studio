## PART 1: Sindh darwaza ban gaya (0:50)

711-712. Muhammad bin Qasim Sindh jeetta hai, Umayyad Khilafat ke liye.

Ab Sindh koi manzil nahi tha. Sindh ek darwaza tha.

Arab lekhak al-Baladhuri likhta hai ki Sindh ke governor Junayd ke daur mein, yaani lagbhag 720 se 740 ke beech, Arab faujein poorab ki taraf badhin. Marwar, Malwa, Gujarat, Ujjain tak chhape pade.

Agla nishana tha poora Uttar Bharat.
[MAP: Sindh to Rajasthan / Malwa / Gujarat arrows. Source on screen: al-Baladhuri.]

Aur yahin, Ujjain-Avanti mein, ek raja khada hota hai.

## PART 2: Nagabhata Pratham, pehli deewar (1:50)

Naam: Nagabhata Pratham. Samay: lagbhag 730 ke aaspaas se 760 tak. [dates differ by source; say "lagbhag"]

Unke vanshaj Mihir Bhoj ka Gwalior shilalekh batata hai ki Nagabhata ne ek taaqatwar "mlechchha" raja ki fauj ko kuchal diya. [SCREEN: "Gwalior inscription"]

Itihaaskaar maante hain ki yahan mlechchha ka matlab Sindh se aaye Arab hain.

Ab ek baat saaf kar doon, kyunki yeh channel sach ke liye hai, tareef ke liye nahi. Us Arab senapati ka naam pakka nahi hai. Itihaaskaar Junayd ka naam lete hain, kuch al-Hakam ka. Aur jo aap social media par "itne hazaar versus itne hazaar" ke aankde dekhte hain, unka koi mazboot source nahi hai. Isliye main woh aankde nahi bolunga.

Aur yeh ladaai akele Nagabhata ki bhi nahi thi. Usi daur mein Gujarat mein Chalukya raja Pulakeshi Avanijanashraya ne bhi Arabon ko peeche dhakela. Navsari ke tamrapatra, lagbhag 739, iske gawah hain. [SCREEN: "Navsari plates c. 739"]

Toh sach yeh hai: raasta kai rajaon ne milkar roka. Par is rukawat ko ek sthaayi samrajya mein badalne ka kaam kisne kiya? Pratiharon ne.

## PART 3: "Pratihar" aur "Gurjar", naam ka raaz (3:00)

Naam mein do hisse hain. Pehle "Pratihar".

Pratihar ka matlab hota hai dwarpal, pehredaar. Is vansh ki apni katha khud ko Ram ke bhai Lakshman se jodti hai, jinhe pratihari kaha gaya. Yeh unke apne abhilekhon ki baat hai.

Ab doosra hissa, "Gurjar".

Padosi rajya aur Arab lekhak inhe Gurjar ke naam se jaante the. Arab vyapari Sulaiman inke rajya ko "Juzr" likhta hai. Aur dasvi sadi ka Rajor abhilekh kehta hai "Gurjara-Pratihara-anvaya".

Ab behes. Kuch itihaaskaar "Gurjar" ko ek samudaay, ek janjaati maante hain. Kuch ise ek ilaake ka naam, Gurjaratra, maante hain.

Yeh behes aaj bhi chal rahi hai. Dadri 2021 aur Kaithal 2023 ke murti vivad aapne sune honge. Isliye main dono paksh rakhunga, faisla nahi sunaunga.

Par ek baat par koi behes nahi hai: is samrajya ne Uttar Bharat ka itihaas badla.

## PART 4: Kannauj, Uttar Bharat ka Rome (4:00)

Ab kahani pashchim se poorab aati hai. Kannauj. Ise Uttar Bharat ki satta ka prateek mana jaata tha.

Iske liye teen taaqatein ladin: Bengal ke Pal, Deccan ke Rashtrakoot, aur Pratihar. Lagbhag do sau saal. Ise itihaas mein tripakshiya sangharsh kehte hain.

## PART 5: Mihir Bhoj, shikhar (5:30)

San 836 se 885. Mihir Bhoj.

Sutlej se Bengal tak. Himalaya se Narmada tak. Us daur ka sabse bada samrajya.
[MAP: Mihir Bhoj's empire]

Aur Arabon ke saath kya hua? Lagbhag 833 se 842 ke beech Bhoj ne Arabon ko Kutch se aur Sindan ke qile se nikaal diya. Iske baad Sindh mein Arab taaqat bikharkar do chhoti riyaasaton mein simat gayi: Multan aur Mansura.

Ab sabse dilchasp gawahi, dushman ki apni.

San 851 ke aaspaas Arab vyapari Sulaiman Bharat aaya. Woh likhta hai ki is raja ke paas bahut badi fauj hai, behtareen ghudsawar hain, aur woh Musalmanon ka kattar dushman hai.
[SCREEN: source line "Sulaiman, c. 851, in Elliot & Dowson, vol. 1". Show as paraphrase, NOT in quotation marks, until the exact wording is checked.]

Sochiye, yeh tareef kisi Bharatiya darbari kavi ki nahi hai. Yeh uski hai, jiske khilaaf woh lad rahe the.

Bhoj ke "Adivaraha" sikke bhi chalte the, Vishnu ke Varaha avatar wale. [IMAGE: Adivaraha coin, free licence only]

## PART 6: Multan ka mandir, woh mod (7:00)

Ab woh mod jiska waada kiya tha.

Multan mein ek vishal Surya mandir tha. Teerth ka bada kendra. Chadhawe se shehar ke shaasak ki sabse badi kamaai hoti thi.

Arab lekhak al-Masudi likhta hai ki jab bhi Bharatiya raja Multan par hamla karte, toh wahan ke Musalman shaasak moorti tod dene ki dhamki dete, aur hamlawar peeche hat jaate. Al-Istakhri bhi us mandir ko poore Sindh aur Hind ke moortipujakon ka sabse bada kendra batata hai.
[SCREEN: "al-Masudi, al-Istakhri". Source: Wikipedia, Multan Sun Temple.]

Yaani ek mandir, fauj ki jagah, dhaal ban chuka tha.

Ek saavdhaani: yeh lekhak kisi ek raja ka naam nahi lete, "padosi Hindu raja" kehte hain. Us waqt Uttar Bharat ki sabse badi Hindu taaqat Pratihar hi the, yeh sandarbh hai, koi uddharan nahi.

## PART 8: Patan (8:45)

San 916. Rashtrakoot raja Indra Tritiya Kannauj par hamla karke use le leta hai. Pratihar lautkar use wapas pa lete hain, par nuksaan ho chuka tha. Chandela, Paramara, Kalachuri jaise saamant ek-ek kar alag hone lage.

Phir san 1018. Mahmud Ghaznavi Kannauj pahunchta hai. Pratihar raja Rajyapala bhaag nikalta hai. Sources ke anusaar, Chandela raja Vidyadhara ne isi karan use saza di. [SCREEN: "1018 Mahmud of Ghazni"]

Aur lagbhag 1036 mein vansh ka ant ho jaata hai.

Dhyaan dijiye: is samrajya ko Arab nahi gira paaye. Giraya ek Turk hamlawar aur ghar ki ladaaiyon ne. Jise bahar se koi nahi tod paaya, woh andar ki phoot se toota.
