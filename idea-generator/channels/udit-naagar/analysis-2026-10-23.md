## 3. Key findings
1. **Your best new video returned to the channel's core.** The Neeraj Pepsu story beat everything else in 3 days. YouTube still links the channel to that audience, and it pushes your videos to them.
2. **Community rivalry and identity topics get the most comments.** The Jat vs Gujjar Bigg Boss short got 44 comments, about 10x any other upload.
3. **National politics and commentary is a crowded space.** CJP worked because it was trending that week. Dhruv Rathee, Gyanesh Kumar and Bishnoi stayed under ~1K because big channels already own those topics.
4. **Random topics with no link to the channel flop.** Examples: the pilot story, plus the biceps and concert shorts.
5. **"Asli kahani / Reality of" is a good format.** Keep it, but point it at people your audience already knows.
6. **Old viral videos still get views.** Add cards, end screens and pinned comments on them that link to the new videos. (Not done yet.)

## 4. Positioning
> **"The stories of Delhi NCR & the Gurjar community: its legends, villages, strongmen, and controversies."** In Hindi, story-driven, 4–7 min.

You own this niche, and no big channel competes in it.

## 6. Shorts strategy
- **Cut every long video into 2–3 shorts**, with the best 30–45s hook and "full story on the channel" at the end.
- **Repost old archive clips** (220kg bench press, Pepsu, wedding entries, Sachin Tanwar push-ups) with a new hook and caption. Those clips already got 40–160K views.
- Keep the **lifestyle shorts** (iPhone, Fold, Gujjar Niwas) to about 1 a week. They do OK and build your personal brand, but they don't grow the niche.
- Stop generic gym and concert shorts.

## 7. Packaging and cadence
- **Titles:** `<Known name> ki asli kahani | <curiosity hook>`, in Hinglish, plus 2–3 hashtags (#gurjar #gujjar #delhincr).
- **Thumbnails:** a big face of the subject, 3–4 words max, high contrast. Use old archive photos of the legends.
- **Comments:** reply to every one in the first hour, and end each video with a question ("Agla video kis legend pe chahiye?"). That gives you free topic research.
- **Cadence:** 2 long videos + 4–5 shorts per week, on the same days each week.
- **Old hits:** add an end screen / card on the 1M Neeraj Pepsu RIP video → new Pepsu story. Do the same on 783K Sanjay Bhati → the new Sanjay Bhati story when it's out.
