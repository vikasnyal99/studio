## What the numbers mean
1. **A Neeraj Pepsu wave is happening right now.** Old Pepsu videos got about 100K views in 28 days. The spike started around Oct 13–15, before the new video, and comes from the Browse feed. The audience is also watching Pepsu song shorts. Ride the wave while it lasts.
2. **Community topics get clicked; national topics don't.** Bishnoi got the most impressions of any new video (22K), but only 2.5% CTR. YouTube tested it and viewers didn't click. Community thumbnails get 6–14% CTR.
3. **Retention is the bottleneck.** About 30–35% average view duration on 5–7 min videos. The first 30 seconds need a stronger hook.
4. **Hardly anyone subscribes, and few viewers return.** +60 subscribers on 137K views, 97.6% new viewers, 3.8% of watch time from subscribers. The old subscribers are mostly inactive. You need series and a reason to come back.
5. **TV is 11% of watch time.** That's a good sign for longer videos.
