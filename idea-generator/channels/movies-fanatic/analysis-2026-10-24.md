4. **Comments come from confusing endings.** The Wailing drew 128 comments, mostly people arguing about the ending.
5. **Retention is low (20–33%).** The scripts started slowly. Put the answer near the start and the explanation after it.

## 6. Copyright rules (important for a film channel)
- Use clips of **10 seconds or less**, mostly stills, trailer frames and your own narration. Don't play long scenes.
- Add commentary on top of every clip. Unaltered film footage gets Content ID claims or strikes.
- Don't use film music in the background. Use the YouTube Audio Library.
- Put the poster and your own face or graphics in the thumbnail. Avoid a plain movie frame.
