## 6. Making videos go viral (rules for every upload)
1. **Pick the topic by checking demand first.** Type the topic into YouTube search, see the autocomplete, and check the age and views of the top results. If big channels posted it this week, find a different angle or skip it.
2. **Package before you script.** Write the title and thumbnail first. If they don't make *you* curious, change the topic.
   - Title: `<Name / Film> + curiosity gap`, in Hinglish, with 1 CAPS word, under 60 characters.
   - Thumbnail: 1 big face or character, 3–4 words maximum, high contrast, the same template every time.
3. **Hook in the first 15 seconds.** Say the most surprising fact or the question first. Never open with "Namaskar doston, aaj hum baat karenge…".
4. **Script structure:** hook → stakes → story → twist → "so what" → a question for the comments.
5. **Publish on time.** Release-week videos need to go up within 24–48 h. Evergreen videos can wait for the best day.
6. **First hour after publishing:** reply to every comment, pin a question, and post a Community post (all 3 channels can).
7. **Cut 2–3 shorts from every long video.** End each short with "poori kahani channel pe".
8. **Copyright (Movies Fanatic and Curious Tail):** clips of 10 seconds or less, always with narration over them, no film music.
