## 2. What the numbers say

### Udit Naagar
1. **The Pepsu wave is still running.** The new Pepsu story went from 6.3K to 8.8K in 6 days without any push. Old wins point the same way: the Fatehpur Beri, wedding and strongman Shorts did 40K–61K (Sachin Tanwar push-ups 61K, Vikas Pardhan 46K/39K, Gujjar wedding dance 40K, Nitin Chandila bench 24K).
2. **The Dhruv Rathee fact-checks worked as Shorts but not as a long video.** The 6 Shorts got 7.6K / 4.9K / 3.3K / 1.8K / 1.7K / 1.2K. The 77-minute-reply long video got 307. This is pan-India politics, which the plan keeps off this channel because CTR was 2.5–3.8%. Shorts reach doesn't bring the NCR audience back, so keep them small or stop.
3. **The phone news Shorts are off-lane.** "Kolhapur Thar" got 498 in about a day. "Bilaspur gold" went up this morning, so it's too early to judge. Neither is an NCR or Gurjar story. Use the same format for NCR / Haryana / West UP news.
4. **Gullu is still in the news.** He revealed a divorce on the show and has a Kanika Mann storyline. Nakul's eviction is still only "reported". Your Nakul vs Gullu Short (7.1K, 9.3% CTR) proves the audience wants this.

### Curious Tail
1. **15 days with no upload, and Ramayana releases in 7 days.** This is the channel the Ramayana "Valmiki vs film" angle was meant for.
2. **Movies Fanatic is doing Curious Tail's job.** The Lakshman Rekha / Valmiki, Ravana's arrow, Sumitra and Jatayu Shorts are mythology stories, not film explainers. Those topics belong on Curious Tail, where the audience (43% female, history and myth) fits.

### Movies Fanatic
1. **The long Ramayana video isn't getting picked up yet** (87 views). That's normal for a channel dead since 2020 (see plan §7), but it means the long video needs search traffic, which only comes in release week.
2. **Story-question Shorts beat trailer-detail Shorts.** "Jatayu ka bada bhai kaun tha?" (1.4K) and "Vajra rishi ki haddiyon se?" (1.2K) did 5–15× better than "Chandrahas naam kyun" (159) or "Ram vs Parashuram" (84).
3. **The release date has changed:** Ramayana Part 1 opens worldwide on **Thu 5 Nov**, with paid previews reported for 4 Nov. It's **not** 8 Nov as the 24 Oct plan says. Advance booking has crossed $2M worldwide. Every Ramayana slot in the plan moves 3 days earlier.
