- The internet is mocking **Adipurush** again, and Manoj Muntashir called it a "failed attempt".
- Some viewers called parts of the teaser's VFX "flat / game-like".

## The rule: don't explain Ramayana, make a *story about* Ramayana
Everyone will post "Ramayana review" and "Ravana explained". Those formats are crowded. What spreads is:
**money**, **comparisons / tier lists**, **"what they didn't show you"**, **what-ifs**, **myth-busting WhatsApp claims**, **nostalgia**, and **predictions people can argue with**.

## 🔍 TRAILER DECODED: hidden details + the mythology behind each shot

### Clues found in the trailers (from published breakdowns; confirm each one frame by frame on the official trailers)
| Trailer clue | Hidden detail (Movies Fanatic) | Mythology behind it (Curious Tail) |
|---|---|---|
| **Ranbir plays Rama + Parashurama + Vishnu (VFX)** | One actor, three roles. Why that's a deliberate choice | Parashurama and Rama are **both avatars of Vishnu**, and they meet after the Shiva-bow scene. Two avatars face each other, and the older one steps aside. This is the strongest hook in the list |
| **The trailer opens on Ravana, not Ram** | Why the makers made the villain the face of the film | Who was Ravana before he became "evil": a scholar, a Shiva devotee, Vishrava's son |
| **Mali** (VFX character) | A character almost nobody recognised | **Mali, Sumali and Malyavan** were rakshasa brothers whom **Vishnu fought and killed** (Uttara Kanda). That fight is the root of the rakshasas' hatred for Vishnu. It suggests the film may show a **prequel war** |
| **Kaikesi (Shobana)**: Ravana's mother | Casting clue: Ravana's backstory is in the film | Sumali married his daughter to sage Vishrava **to produce a son powerful enough to defeat the gods**. Ravana was "planned" |
| **Indra + Airavat** (new in the international trailer) | A scene that's only in the international cut | Ravana's family **defeated Indra**, and that's how Meghnad got the name **"Indrajit"**. Where is Meghnad in the trailers? (a missing-character hook) |
| **Ravana praying before a giant Shiva idol, fire rising** (international) | A new scene that ties into the Ravana Tandav song | **Ravana lifting Kailash**, Shiva pressing it down with his toe, and Ravana singing the **Shiv Tandav Stotram** in pain. That's how he got the name "Ravana" (the one who roars) |
| **Pushpak Vimaan + Jatayu's longer fight** (international) | How the aerial fight was shot (VFX); Jatayu is voiced by Anupam Kher | **Pushpak originally belonged to Kubera**, Ravana's half-brother, and Ravana took it from him. And the story of Jatayu and Sampati flying toward the sun |
| **Golden Lanka, palace gates, glowing skyline** | The production design | **Who built the golden Lanka?** Vishwakarma built it for Kubera, and Ravana took it. A folk version says it was Shiva's palace for Parvati |
| **Vivek Oberoi as Vidyutjihva** (brief cameo) | A cameo almost nobody spotted | **Ravana killed his own sister Shurpanakha's husband.** Did Shurpanakha lead Ravana to his doom on purpose, as revenge? (a theory from later retellings) |
| **Sita holding a weapon** | A new take on Sita | The tradition of **Sita lifting Shiva's bow as a child**, which is why Janaka set the bow test (later tradition, not Valmiki) |
| **Arun Govil as Dashrath** | 1987's Ram is now Ram's father, a handoff between the two versions | **Dashrath's curse**: the Shravan Kumar story. Dashrath dies of grief for his son, just as Shravan's parents did |
| **Marich + the golden deer; Subahu revealed** | The deer VFX | **Marich and Subahu had met Rama before**, at Vishwamitra's yagna. Rama killed Subahu and threw Marich 100 yojanas away with one arrow. That's why Marich feared Rama |
| **Kumbhakarna glimpsed in the war flashes** | Part 2 teaser shots inside the Part 1 trailer | The **boon that went wrong**: Saraswati turned "Indrasan" into "Nidrasan", so he got sleep instead of Indra's throne |
| **No Hanuman, no Vanar Sena** | Where Part 1 probably ends (the abduction of Sita) | Who Hanuman is before he meets Ram |

## 🚫 Red lines (to keep a viral video from turning into an FIR or a strike)
- Never mock Ram, Sita or Hanuman, or devotion itself. Debate *portrayals and the film*, not the faith. "Tacky" means the packaging is loud, never the respect.
- Label what's Valmiki, what's later tradition, and what's folk belief. This audience fact-checks in the comments.
- No pirated or cam footage, no leaked scenes, and no fake "leak" thumbnails.
- Use clips of 10 seconds or less with narration over them. Don't put the film's songs (Ravana Tandav and others) under your video; use YouTube Audio Library tracks.
