# Storyboard sheets (the close-up method)

One ChatGPT generation = one 2x2 sheet = 4 shots. A 20-25 s Short needs 4 sheets (16 shots).
Send them one after another **in the same chat** that already has the character pose sheet, so faces stay the same.
Order each sheet like the story: sheet 1 set-up, 2 first exchange, 3 escalation, 4 finish.

## Header (paste at the start of every sheet; fill the [..])

```
New image, a comic storyboard sheet, same characters and same flat 2D Indian animated-series cel-shaded style as the pose sheet above
([CHARACTER A: look in one line]. [CHARACTER B: look in one line]).
Setting: [place in one line]. PORTRAIT 1024x1536 canvas split into a 2x2 grid of 4 equal vertical panels separated by thin pure white gutters.
No text, no captions, no speech bubbles. Cinematic cartoon camera angles, dramatic lighting, bold outlines. Cartoon violence only, no blood, no gore.
```

## Panel menu (mix far, close, face, impact; one idea per panel)

- wide establishing shot from behind a pillar, characters small
- extreme close-up of A's angry face, eyebrows low, veins, glaring at camera
- close-up of B's face: smirk turning alert / shocked / panicked
- low-angle shot of B charging at the camera, speed lines
- extreme close-up of a fist flying at the camera
- close-up of the face being punched: head snapped, cheek squashed, sweat, impact star
- over-the-shoulder shot: B counter-punches, A unaffected
- block smashed: crossed forearms, cracks and shockwave rings
- grab by the front of the vest, faces inches apart
- flurry of body punches with ghost fists and several impact stars
- extreme close-up of eyes, fist pulled back (tension before the finish)
- finishing punch on the jaw, huge impact burst (no blood)
- wide shot: victim flying backwards, speed lines
- ending: victim sitting holding the jaw, dizzy stars, A standing out of focus behind

Write each as `PANEL 1 (top-left): ...` `PANEL 2 (top-right): ...` `PANEL 3 (bottom-left): ...` `PANEL 4 (bottom-right): ...`.

Worked example (4 sheets, Neeraj vs boxer in a hotel parking): see `jobs/2026-10-23-dev-tanwar-short/storyboard.md`.
