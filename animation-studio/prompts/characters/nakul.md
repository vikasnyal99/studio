# NAKUL — unbeaten boxer (Lily White Hotel teaser)

From the audio: "vikat tagda ladka... bahut tagda boxer... ab tak na pita".
Design idea: athletic boxer (leaner and lighter than Neeraj so the size difference
reads instantly), dressed for a Holi party.

Step 1: generate **Prompt K1** (the master sheet). When it looks right, save it as
`assets/incoming/nakul_sheet.png`. From then on, attach it for every Nakul prompt.

## CHARACTER DNA (paste unchanged)

```
CHARACTER — NAKUL:
Indian man, about 24. Athletic boxer build: lean and very defined, broad shoulders,
visible abs, shorter and slimmer than a bodybuilder (clearly less bulky).
Light wheatish skin. Short black hair, skin-fade on the sides, slightly spiky on top.
Sharp face, light stubble, confident cocky expression, small scar through the
left eyebrow. Thin silver chain.
Clothes: tight white sleeveless gym vest splashed with pink, green and yellow
Holi colour powder; navy-blue track shorts with two white side stripes;
red boxing hand wraps on both hands; white sports shoes.
```

6 poses left to right, labelled:

2 "JAB" — left arm fully extended forward at face height
3 "CHARGE" — running forward, leaning in, fists up
4 "HIT" — head snapped back, chin up, body bending backwards, arms flung out,
