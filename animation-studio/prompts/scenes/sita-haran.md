## CHARACTER DNA

- SITA: young Indian woman, gentle graceful face, large calm eyes, long black hair in a single braid with small white flowers,
  simple saffron-yellow cotton sari with a thin red border, small red bindi, a few simple gold bangles, barefoot, forest-dweller simplicity.
- RAVANA AS SAGE: tall lean old ascetic, long grey matted hair tied in a top knot, long grey beard, saffron robe and shawl,
  rudraksha beads, wooden kamandalu (water pot) and a wooden staff, bare feet; kind smile but sharp, cunning dark eyes.
- RAVANA TRUE FORM: towering powerful king, ten crowned heads (one central head, four on each side, slightly smaller, in a row),
  tall ornate gold crown on the central head, thick black moustache, dark heavy eyebrows, fierce dark-red eyes,
  deep maroon and black royal clothes with heavy gold armour ornaments, jewelled necklaces, broad shoulders.
