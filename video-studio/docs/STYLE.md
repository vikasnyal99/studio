# Style

Reference channels: Nitish Rajput, Dhruv Rathee, Think School. Agreed with
the user on 12 Oct 2026 after the first full video.

## On-screen text

The user's rule (13 Oct 2026, after watching the first full video): **the
screen must not read like subtitles.** The voice explains; the screen shows.
Viewers should never have to read a sentence.

- Headings only: two to four words, the name of the idea being discussed.
  "Quota <b>protest</b>", not "Student protest: government jobs mein quota
  system".
- Where a sentence would go, put an animation or more pictures instead:
  `icons`, `collage`, `timeline`, `balance`, `loop`, `spread`, or a `map`
  with camera moves.
- Roman Hinglish, the way the user types it. No Devanagari on screen.
- Classy and not very big. A small gold label above one calm line.
- One accent: gold, through `<b>...</b>`, on the one word that matters.
- Labels above photos say what the photo really is and when
  ("Colombo · 13 April 2022"), not what the narration is arguing.
- Write wording from the script (or transcript meaning), in clean spelling.
  Never copy Whisper's Devanagari spellings.

## Picture choices, best first

1. The user's own footage or photos
2. A real photo of the thing being discussed, or a `collage` of several
3. A screenshot of the article, post or page that proves the point
4. A `map` (places, routes, who borders whom, who is watching whom)
5. An animation: `icons`, `timeline`, `loop`, `balance`, `spread`,
   `network`, `chain`, `number`
6. `title`: a short question or term, a few times per video
7. `words` and sentence-length `pills`: do not use

## Pace and motion

- A new scene every 3 to 8 seconds, cut on the first word of the new idea.
- Long explanations of geography work best as one map scene with several
  camera moves and changing captions.
- End each story with a `chain` that names its sequence.

## Maps

- Gold for the country being discussed, muted blue (`"blue"`) for India,
  muted red (`"red"`) for China or the rival power.
- India's borders come from the India-view Natural Earth file; do not swap it.

## Opening (first 12 s)

The user disliked a slow start (one panel for 5 s). Cold open: something new on screen every 2 to 3 s, a flash
on cut-in, a shake and flash on a gunshot. For a crime story the `comic` opener with date, subject, place and
"unknown gunmen" silhouettes walking in then BANG worked and was liked. Speaker appears about when the narration says "ab".

## Sound

- Templates add their own effects; add `sfx` only for a deliberate accent.
- **No whoosh on every picture** (the user found it irritating). `Scene.cue` keeps whooshes only for the opening,
  `title`, `map`, `recon` or scenes marked `loud`; elsewhere every second whoosh becomes a quiet pop/tick/click.
  The Bishnoi video went from 50 whooshes to 7.
- Music sits about 13 dB under the voice (the user wanted it audible; 19 was too faint). Change track at big section breaks.
  With the punchy voice chain (make-video skill, "Quality and sound") 10 dB under felt more energetic (24 Oct 2026).
- Tracks in `library/music/` need the credit lines that
  `output/description_credits.txt` prints.

## Honesty on screen

- The screen states only what was verified. If the narration attributes a
  claim to an outlet you could not find, show the outlet you did find, or a
  neutral graphic, and tell the user so they can re-record if needed.
- Allegations stay worded as allegations.
