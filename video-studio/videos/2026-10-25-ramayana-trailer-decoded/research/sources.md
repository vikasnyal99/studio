Wikipedia says "set to be the most expensive Indian film ever made", an estimate.

Confirmed 2026-10-26 for the current script
- Payasam: Kaushalya half, Sumitra a quarter, Kaikeyi an eighth, Sumitra another eighth (Bala 16 prose and commentary): https://www.valmikiramayan.net/utf8/baala/sarga16/bala_16_prose.htm
- Kailash (Uttara 16): Shiva pressed the mountain with his toe ("padangushthena ... pidayam asa lilaya"), Ravana's hands were crushed under it, he cried out, Shiva named him Ravana and gave the khadga Chandrahasa: Sanskrit lines in the Kamakoti Uttara Ramayana PDF above.
